# Issue #43 — omitted-axis candidate and edge reconciliation

**Research status, 2026-09-26:** Bounded fixture comparison and owner visual check complete. The owner corrected an initial left-corner concern, then rechecked empty spaces, buildings, and roads and reported that the candidate matches the live city. Game serializer evidence remains unavailable. The zero-axis rule is a strong **fixture-specific inference**, not an accepted source contract. This report is for [issue #43](https://github.com/FoE-City-Optimizer/city-optimizer/issues/43) under M1 Epic #22.

## A. Research question

For the reviewed #24 current main-city fixture, does an omitted entity or unlocked-area axis mean grid coordinate zero, and does applying that interpretation reconcile the edge geometry without concealing contradictions?

## B. Executive conclusion

The zero candidate positions **399/399** retained ordinary entities and **234/234** unlocked rectangles; the **124/124** explicit 1×1 roads do not move. Its area union is **3,984** cells and its ordinary footprints occupy **3,970** distinct cells, with **zero overlap** and **zero outside-area cells**. This is internally consistent with 14 conditional free cells. The counterexample that fills omissions with one produces **84 overlapping footprint cells** and an area union of **3,900** cells. Confidence is high in those calculations for the exact reviewed fixture and high in the candidate’s visual match to this owner’s city. The owner rechecked empty spaces, buildings, and roads and called it “100% my city.” The actual game serializer rule is still not independently proven.

The in-scope geometry exit is supported for this tested city and capture variant: the candidate has positive joined ordinary footprints, zero overlap/outside placement, all roads unchanged, and owner confirmation of the edge view. The owner comparison is qualitative rather than a saved tile-by-tile oracle. The 20 special/off-grid entries remain aggregate-only in the approved fixture; their individual visible-grid status is not proven, so this is not a universal or solver-valid full-city contract.

## E. Findings

**Verified facts from the approved projection.** The 419 source entities reconcile as 399 retained ordinary records and 20 aggregate-only special/off-grid records: `hub_main` 2, `hub_part` 5, `friends_tavern` 1, `off_grid` 10, and `outpost_ship` 2. The 399 ordinary records resolve to positive joined footprints. Exactly 21 ordinary records lack one axis (10 x, 11 y); exactly 21 of 234 unlocked rectangles lack one axis (10 x, 11 y). None of those entities or rectangles has an explicit zero on either axis. All 124 road positions are explicit. The 49 blocked-position records have 14 incomplete positions and unknown semantics.

**Verified candidate arithmetic.** With a provisional zero for only missing axes, all 399 ordinary records and 234 rectangles are positioned. Their 3,984-area-cell union contains all 3,970 unique occupied ordinary cells, with no ordinary overlap. The 49 blocked positions become unique, and none is inside that conditional area union; this locates them but does not establish that they are unbuildable. With a provisional one, 84 ordinary footprint cells overlap. Both candidates retain 124 unchanged road cells. The one candidate's 14 apparent free cells are not validation because of its overlaps.

**Likely observation, not verified serializer fact.** The strict absence of explicit zero across entities and rectangles, edge placement under the zero candidate, the clean occupancy fit, and FoE Helper's independent consumer fallback collectively support zero suppression for this capture. The actual game serializer or a controlled live zero/nonzero transition has not been inspected. The old Chrome 154.0.8037.58 and `merged-game-c3b9b79d.js` fingerprint identify the capture variant, not a universal game build.

**Inference.** The zero candidate is suitable for owner review as a local research visualization. The exact field-presence pattern must remain available to any later adapter; a changed omission set, unknown class/definition, or source fingerprint must stop automatic inference.

**Owner visual correction.** An initial comparison appeared to show missing unlocked rectangles and buildings in the two left corners. The owner explicitly withdrew that finding, rechecked empty spaces, buildings, and roads, and reported full visual agreement. It is not retained as a mismatch or used to infer missing source records.

## G. Contradictions discovered

The one-axis fallback contradicts the nonoverlap expected of an observed ordinary city arrangement in 84 cell instances. The previous partial viewer's 49 occupied cells outside its 3,648-cell explicitly positioned area union are covered under the zero candidate; they were not evidence of invalid placement. The owner withdrew the initial left-corner mismatch. No accepted ADR or charter assumption is contradicted. The fixture cannot support treating omitted fields as explicitly present zeros.

## H. Remaining uncertainty

- The owner reports full visual agreement for empty spaces, buildings, and roads after correcting an initial left-corner impression. The comparison is qualitative and does not certify each of the 21 newly shown records tile by tile.
- The relevant game-side serialization has not been independently inspected. A client asset fingerprint is not a source schema contract.
- The 20 excluded entries are only class aggregates. Earlier conditional arithmetic placed their source footprints outside unlocked cells, but the approved fixture cannot reveal whether an excluded entry is a visible in-grid object needed for a complete city view. The visual claim stays partial until that question is answered safely.
- Blocked-position meaning, road-demand rules, functional connectivity, and validity of a rearranged or optimal layout are outside this evidence.
- A later capture or different city/world may have a different omission pattern or source variant.

## I. Project impact

The experiment provides #43 evidence for [#48](https://github.com/FoE-City-Optimizer/city-optimizer/issues/48): a scoped zero-axis candidate with exact counts, negative cases, and owner visual confirmation. Architecture & Planning must decide whether this is sufficient to accept the mapping at the contract/domain boundary. The 20 excluded classes and blocked-position semantics limit the completeness claim and later solver eligibility. The production collector, Snapshot v1, API, and solver are unchanged. Privacy exposure is limited to the already approved pseudonymous fixture and an owner-local generated SVG; no raw capture, identifiers, sessions, upload, or gameplay automation occurred. Any new capture, changed fixture, or publication of the SVG needs an exact-artifact Security & Privacy review.

Use the zero-axis derivative as an owner-verified research view for the exact reviewed fixture, with the omission state preserved in the source artifact.
