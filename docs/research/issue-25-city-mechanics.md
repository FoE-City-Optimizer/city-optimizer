# Issue #25 — main-city mechanics and safe treatment

**Status:** Research conclusion, 2026-09-25. **Confidence:** high for the reviewed replay's selected fields and current official rules cited below; low for omitted source-field meanings and individual special relationships. This is a policy proposal, not Snapshot v1, a solver rule, or acceptance of proposed ADR-011.

## A. Question and B. conclusion

Which current main-city mechanics can the first ordinary-city model support, and what must it do when a mechanic or source-field meaning is uncertain?

The #24 case supports a **geometry-only, conditional** replay of 399 retained entries, five observed classes, 135 joined footprints, 124 explicit 1×1 streets, one positioned 6×7 `main_building` candidate, and the recorded unlocked/blocked candidates. It does **not** support a full functional city or optimality claim. All three coordinate-bearing source collections have omissions; generic street requirements also have omissions; blocked-position meaning and special relationships remain unknown. A planner may show the observed current geometry with explicit uncertainty, but must reject compilation to a full-validity solver input when a critical unknown affects the modeled city. A narrower solver input is conceivable only after a later authority approves its eligibility boundary and source-semantic proofs.

## F. Ordinary-city eligibility and conformance proposal

**Proposed eligibility, not a project decision:** A candidate instance may enter a geometry display if it has a retained observed ordinary class (`generic_building`, `greatbuilding`, `street`, `main_building`, `military`), a unique within-snapshot instance, a resolved definition, positive fixed footprint, and explicit coordinates; unresolved entries are shown as diagnostics rather than silently positioned. A _functional_ solver input additionally needs independently established available/blocked semantics, Town Hall identity, road type/connectivity and per-definition demand, plus a reliable way to detect set/chain/adjacency behavior even inside `generic_building`. The current #24 city does not meet that gate. The 20 excluded entries remain count-only outside the candidate geometry; unknown new classes reject capture. No first-model claim should imply that all generic buildings are ordinary.

Positive cases supported by the reviewed replay: exact instance/definition join; unique IDs; legacy and generic positive footprint paths; explicit 1×1 roads; one candidate root with 6×7 footprint; explicit coordinate preservation; exact omission counts; exclusion census; conditional no-overlap check. Negative cases to derive from **in-memory mutations of a copy**, never a replacement approved fixture: missing/duplicate join; nonpositive or ambiguous footprint; new class; absent coordinate where resolved geometry is required; replacing an explicit `1` road field with absence; changing an absent field to `0`; treating a street definition's level as a building demand; introducing a 2×2 road into a single-lane-only validator; changing special class counts without changing total. Expect rejection or an explicit `uncertain`/`unsupported` diagnostic, never a full-validity pass. For coordinate semantics, roadless mapping, double-road topology, Town Hall universality, blocked overlap semantics, set/chain effects, and adjacency bonuses, **no safe positive conformance fixture exists yet**. Do not invent one or assert a functionally valid output.

## G. Contradictions, H. unknowns, and I. project impact

No Accepted ADR is contradicted. The fixture contradicts the implicit idea that every critical source value is explicit. Current official support invalidates assuming every building needs a road or that all roads are interchangeable 1×1 segments. The #24 special census invalidates treating the entire 419-entry source as ordinary solver occupancy. The project charter's grid model remains a hypothesis for applicable main-city entities, not a proven universal validity oracle.

Unknowns that cannot be closed from this privacy-reviewed artifact: source omission rules for all three coordinate collections; blocked-area meaning, especially overlap cases; metadata interpretation for absent street demand; street level as road supply versus demand; live two-lane source representation; universal Town Hall discriminator; per-entity chain/set/adjacency identities and effects; whether generic definitions conceal these effects; unusual move/rotation/placement limits; another city/world/build variant. A new owner-authorized observation may answer narrow gaps only after its own allowlist and exact-artifact privacy review. This research does not retain raw or private data.

For [#26](https://github.com/FoE-City-Optimizer/city-optimizer/issues/26), the reviewed replay is a conformance source for field presence, joins, shapes, exclusions, and negative handling, **not** a ready Snapshot v1 instance. Snapshot design can specify `unknown`/unsupported diagnostics and rejection boundaries, but cannot normatively assign `0`, `NONE`, or blocked subtraction. M2 solver input is gated on a resolved eligible subset and explicit semantics; otherwise results may claim only conditional geometric consistency. Proposed ADR-011 needs a policy decision before any chain/set-sensitive production result. ADR-005 and ADR-006 remain intact; no production collector or upload is authorized by this report. The security posture retains strict projection, no raw archive, no individual special records in public research, no session data, and new privacy review for any changed fixture bytes.

## J. ADR-011 options and recommendation

| Option                                                                                | Advantage                               | Risk / required decision                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reject cities with any unresolved special or critical source field                    | Simple, honest full-validity boundary   | Very narrow coverage; Architecture & Planning must define diagnostics and eligibility.                        |
| Freeze verified special groups and optimize an independently verified ordinary subset | Can produce bounded plans later         | Requires reliable membership/topology and proof that moves do not alter effects; current replay lacks this.   |
| Allow geometry-only preview with manual review                                        | Useful now for observation and research | Must label every result conditional and prohibit “valid/optimal” and benefit claims; no solver-valid handoff. |
| Implement full chain/set/adjacency model                                              | Potential broader coverage              | Needs new safe examples, authoritative metadata, and dedicated tests; not an M1 assumption.                   |

**Research recommendation:** Use the geometry-only preview policy for this evidence set, with reject-on-unknown compilation to functional solver input. Architecture & Planning should amend or decide ADR-011 after defining the eligible subset and proof standard. This does not accept an ADR or prescribe Snapshot v1 fields.
