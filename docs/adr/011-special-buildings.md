# ADR-011: Chains, sets, and adjacency-sensitive buildings

**Status:** Proposed

## Context

Do not silently claim functional validity based on geometric checks alone.

## Decision / proposal

Research the mechanics, preserve critical relationships or flag manual review until modeled.

## Consequences and next evidence

Keep implementation within this scope. Any unspecified framework, schema detail, or operational choice requires evidence and a scoped Issue before acceptance. See the [charter](../project/project-charter.md).
