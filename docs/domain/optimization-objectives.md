# Optimization objectives

Hard validity and Town Hall road connectivity come first. Then minimize road tiles. At a fixed proven or best-known road count, improve free-space usefulness; a later tie-breaker may reduce disruption. Reports must label `OPTIMAL`, `BEST KNOWN / FEASIBLE`, `INFEASIBLE`, or `UNKNOWN / TERMINATED` accurately. The free-space metric and solver formulation remain research decisions. See [charter](../project/project-charter.md) and [ADR-010](../adr/010-objectives.md).
