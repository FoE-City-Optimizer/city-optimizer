# Current-city geometry derivation

Issue [#48](https://github.com/FoE-City-Optimizer/city-optimizer/issues/48) derives a **geometry-only city draft** from the unchanged, privacy-approved [#24 source projection](../../tests/fixtures/issue-24-reference-replay.json). The coordinate rule is the owner-accepted, source-variant-scoped decision in [ADR-017](../adr/017-current-city-coordinate-mapping.md). [ADR-016](../adr/016-snapshot-authority.md) governs the draft's JSON Schema authority. This draft is neither Canonical City Snapshot v1 nor solver input.

## Mapping and bounds

| Source projection                                      | City geometry draft                                     | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `entities[].instance`, `.entity`, `.type`              | `ordinaryInstances[].instance`, `.entity`, `.type`      | Within-capture pseudonyms and five ordinary classes only. Duplicate instances, unsupported classes, and unknown definitions reject.                                                         |
| `entities[].x`, `.y`                                   | `ordinaryInstances[].x`, `.y`, `xPresence`, `yPresence` | Integer city-grid cells, x east/right, y south/down. A listed #24 omission maps to zero with `omitted-zero` presence. An explicit zero retains `explicit` presence. A new omission rejects. |
| `definitions[].width`, `.length` or `.sizeX`, `.sizeY` | `ordinaryInstances[].width`, `.height`                  | Exactly one positive footprint form per definition; x dimension first. Every retained entity joins a definition.                                                                            |
| `unlockedAreas[].x`, `.y`, `.width`, `.length`         | `unlockedRectangles[]`                                  | Same axes and presence rule. Rectangle overlap is allowed; the union defines the observed available area for this case.                                                                     |
| `blockedAreas[]`                                       | `blockedRecords[]`                                      | Preserve field presence and explicit values. No blocked cell is subtracted from the available union because meaning is unresolved.                                                          |
| `excludedCounts`                                       | `excludedCounts`                                        | Five aggregate-only special/off-grid classes; no individual position or eligibility is inferred.                                                                                            |

The [source-projection parser](../../packages/contracts/src/source-city-projection.ts) rejects unexpected fields, invalid or newly omitted coordinates, changed observation fingerprint, unsupported classes, duplicate pseudonyms, unresolved definitions, and count drift. Its exact 399/234/49 array lengths and numeric bounds are scoped to the approved fixture; they are **not product limits**. The [domain derivation](../../packages/domain/src/city-geometry.ts) checks no ordinary footprint overlap or outside-area cell, 124 explicit 1×1 roads, and the observed 3,984 available / 3,970 occupied / 14 free-cell census. The source's 419 records reconcile as 399 ordinary and 20 aggregate-only exclusions. Area and occupancy geometry are proved only for this case; special entries may affect a later functional city model.

The draft retains `BLOCKED_MEANING_UNRESOLVED`, `ROAD_DEMAND_UNRESOLVED`, and `SPECIAL_ELIGIBILITY_UNRESOLVED`. No consumer should turn it into a solver-valid or optimal claim. A different capture, city, source fingerprint, or omission pattern needs a new compatibility decision and privacy review before use.

## Conformance

The single wire-shape authority is [city-geometry.schema.json](../../packages/contracts/src/city-geometry.schema.json); TypeScript declarations are generated from it. Ajv Draft 2020-12 validates the TypeScript output, and Python `jsonschema` Draft 2020-12 validates the same in-memory 16-case corpus. The full-city positive case derives from the hash-checked #24 fixture; a separate wire-only positive checks explicit zero. Negative cases cover unknown versions, missing/invalid geometry, presence mismatches, unknown fields/classes, count drift, and missing diagnostics. Domain tests separately cover overlap, outside placement, duplicate IDs, unknown definitions, unsupported source-axis omissions, unsupported classes, and source-variant drift.

Run `pnpm check`, then `node tests/city-geometry-cases.mjs | uv run --project tests/conformance --locked --python 3.12 python tests/conformance/validate.py`. The corpus travels through a local process pipe and is not committed as a second city artifact. CI runs both validators. The [original fixture publication review](../research/issue-24-privacy-review.md) applies to the unchanged fixture bytes; it does not authorize new captures or publication of a city SVG.
