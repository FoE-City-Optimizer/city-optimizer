# City model baseline

The city is a set of available integer-grid cells, with optional blocked cells. Building instances have fixed rectangular footprints and road requirement `NONE`, `SINGLE`, or `DOUBLE`; the Town Hall roots the road network. Orthogonal adjacency counts. Buildings and roads may not overlap or leave available space. MVP is expected to support `NONE` and `SINGLE`; handling of `DOUBLE` requires a separate decision. Chain, set, and adjacency-sensitive effects need evidence and a policy before production results. These are model intentions from the [charter](../project/project-charter.md), not a finalized schema. Research observations in [initial notes](../research/initial-research-notes.md) must be revalidated.
