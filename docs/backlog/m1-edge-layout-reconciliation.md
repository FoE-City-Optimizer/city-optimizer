## What the evidence says

The #40 replay places **378/399 ordinary instances** and **213/234 unlocked rectangles** because 21 of each omit at least one coordinate. It places **124/124 roads**. The explicitly positioned areas cover 3,648 cells; 49 occupied footprint cells lie outside that partial area union. These 49 cells may be inside the 21 omitted-position areas. The earlier [#24](../research/issue-24-real-city-fixture.md) and [#25](../research/issue-25-city-mechanics.md) diagnostic that assigns zero to omitted axes yields 3,984 candidate unlocked cells, 3,970 ordinary occupied cells, 14 candidate free cells, and no ordinary overlap or outside placement. That arithmetic and the owner's edge observation support a specific hypothesis: an omitted source axis represents zero for this source variant. They do not independently establish it.

FoE Helper is a **comparison source**, not our adapter or an authority for game semantics. At its 2026-09-15 [commit `2e50d22`](https://github.com/mainIine/foe-helfer-extension/tree/2e50d22ef1880f3ad02589a569fc4fd288925bcb), its [building display](https://github.com/mainIine/foe-helfer-extension/blob/2e50d22ef1880f3ad02589a569fc4fd288925bcb/js/web/citymap/js/parts/city-buildings.js#L1664-L1674) substitutes zero for missing entity axes, and its [city-builder renderer](https://github.com/mainIine/foe-helfer-extension/blob/2e50d22ef1880f3ad02589a569fc4fd288925bcb/js/web/city-builder/js/parts/renderer.js#L115-L139) uses a zero fallback for area bounds. Its [main-city parser](https://github.com/mainIine/foe-helfer-extension/blob/2e50d22ef1880f3ad02589a569fc4fd288925bcb/js/web/_main/js/_main.js#L316-L325) passes source entities and areas through; the fallback is a consumer choice. Another [grid-rendering path](https://github.com/mainIine/foe-helfer-extension/blob/2e50d22ef1880f3ad02589a569fc4fd288925bcb/js/web/citymap/js/citymap.js#L288-L326) does not make that fallback explicit. This is corroboration for a test, not proof. FoE Helper is AGPL-3.0; no code or data should be copied into this project. Its [road-demand handling](https://github.com/mainIine/foe-helfer-extension/blob/2e50d22ef1880f3ad02589a569fc4fd288925bcb/js/web/city-builder/js/city-builder.js#L577-L590) also uses heuristic defaults, so it cannot settle our road rules by itself.

## Acceptance target for the current main-city grid

“Complete geometry” means that, for the tested city/source variant, all **399/399 retained ordinary instances** and **234/234 unlocked rectangles** have evidence-backed grid positions and positive joined footprints; all **124/124 roads** stay aligned; the area and occupancy comparison has no unexplained overlap or out-of-area footprint; and the owner confirms the previously missing edge structures against the live city. The 20 excluded special/off-grid records must reconcile to the 419-entry source and be explicitly classified. If any is a visible in-grid object needed for a complete city view, the result remains partial until its geometry and privacy treatment are resolved. Blocked-area semantics, street demand, and functional road validity are distinct from visual geometry and must not be inferred from a correct-looking map.

This target is scoped to the observed main city and source variant. A future build/city still requires an adapter compatibility check. “100% accurate” must not mean a universal FoE schema guarantee or a solver-valid/optimal plan.
